Chest BPM 1.5 (7) — local heart-sound timing research, 2026-11-14

What ships
BeatStates.mlpackage is our small causal convolutional sound-state model, trained from scratch on this Mac. It predicts noise/unannotated, S1, systole, S2 and diastole from eight energy features. It does not predict AFib, murmurs, disease or probability of health. No personal recordings, Apple Watch readings or participant identities were used in training. Watch data never enters the estimator. Core ML executes on the phone.

Feature schema: chestbpm-energy-v1. Native Swift features matched the Python implementation exactly on 400 test frames (max difference 0). Native model logits differed from PyTorch by at most 1.90735e-6 on the conversion check. These checks establish conversion consistency, not clinical accuracy. Model metadata includes schema and training-weight SHA256. algorithm-freeze.json pins the exact source and deployed package before the held-out evaluation.

Data and attribution
CirCor DigiScope Phonocardiogram Dataset 1.0.3, Oliveira et al. (2022), PhysioNet, https://doi.org/10.13026/tshs-mw03 . Open Data Commons Attribution License v1.0. Original publication: Oliveira et al., The CirCor DigiScope Dataset: From Murmur Detection to Murmur Classification, IEEE JBHI, https://doi.org/10.1109/JBHI.2021.3137048 . PhysioNet platform citation is listed on the dataset page. The public dataset license is cached with the data, and individual WAV/TSV checksums are recorded in manifest.json. Dataset labels concern source participants; this app does not implement a murmur classifier.

The manifest contains 120 linked-patient groups. Additional ID links are resolved before splitting. 80 groups train, 16 development groups validate, 12 originally frozen groups form the initial test. After an engineering test exposed a transient-mask recovery bug, twelve additional untouched groups were reserved as final_test. Neither test cohort shares patients with development or with the other test cohort. The first cohort remains reported separately; the one-line fix and old hashes are preserved in mask-recovery-change.txt and initial-algorithm-freeze.json. No model weights or decoder thresholds changed after the initial evaluation. No test recordings are loaded by training. The 12 test groups were not used to choose decoder settings. Four development and four test groups per murmur-label stratum were selected first; development only expanded to 32 per stratum. These small subsets are not representative population validation. CirCor used clinical recording equipment; this is a major mismatch with a bare iPhone microphone.

Augmentation: unmodified/gain, white noise, hum, VOiCES speech and babble, synthetic taps and contact loss. The two short VOiCES examples are the official PyTorch audio augmentation tutorial examples, https://docs.pytorch.org/audio/main/tutorials/audio_data_augmentation_tutorial.html , originally VOiCES (CC BY 4.0, IARPA/Lab41/SRI; see https://iqtlabs.github.io/voices/images/VOiCES_eval_plan.v6.pdf ). Exact download URLs and SHA256 are pinned in fetch_assets.py. The same speech/babble source clips are reused for training and test mixtures; this is not unseen-noise-source validation. Short clips repeat with boundary fades. Independent synthetic controls use a new fixed seed.

Reference BPM comes from semi-supervised public S1 annotation onset intervals, not simultaneous ECG. Missing annotations are reported, not filled in. The word "clean" in generated case IDs means the original unmodified file; it does not assert noise-free audio. Waveform similarity to an unmodified file is not proof of clinical denoising quality.

Denoiser research
A separate experimental importer evaluated LU-Net's published HDF5 models, authors' repository https://github.com/ShamsNafisaAli/LU-Net-Heart-Sound-Denoising- , commit ffb0d6b7ad901ea5e6fe9140a6f935b6a4231fe4, paper IEEE 10173517. LU-Net HDF5 SHA256: 7fccca690c7e9c0f9f14d619b6d1b8e2f55ebc8a0dae813b151533c5fb647ca0. Independent NumPy/PyTorch and native conversion checks were below 5e-7. The denoiser increased returned errors on development cases, so it is NOT bundled or enabled in the app. Source has an MIT header, but pretrained-weight redistribution terms were not clear enough to publish the weight file. Downloads/converted experiments remain in ignored local cache/generated directories. No claim is made that its historical training data are independent of our benchmark. Our deployed timing model uses no LU-Net weights or copied architecture implementation.

Springer/Schmidt reference code (https://physionet.org/content/hss/1.0/, GPLv3) was read for research; no code or trained weights were copied. The app's band features, masking, model, decoder and checks are our implementation.

Murmur prototype 1.8 (13), 2026-11-14: ML/Models/Murmur.mlpackage is a separate small model trained from scratch using CirCor murmur annotations. It does not reuse the timing model's weights or require its BPM decision. The complete waveform-to-log-mel frontend is exported in Core ML. The target is Absent/Present/Unknown sound labels; scores are not probabilities of disease. No Watch, private audio, personal recording or clinical-outcome label trains it. Additional-ID grouping splits 872 linked people into 652 training,100 validation and120 final-test groups; fresh final groups exclude all156 previously accessed BPM groups. Location-specific annotations prevent assigning a Present patient's every recording a positive label. Unknown remains uncertain. Runtime, weights, code, metrics and native executable were frozen before final waveform download; all source WAVs match published checksums. Results, baseline, missed cases, false alarms, uncertain cases and phone-transfer limits are in Research/murmur-validation-v18.txt and ML/murmur-report.json. This new test cohort is now consumed. Three public validation samples, selected alphabetically per class before checking correctness, are bundled under MurmurSamples with their attribution, checksums and full data license. They are truncated to at most20seconds. The app performs no uploads. This prototype does not diagnose valve conditions or AFib, and its live phone murmur accuracy is unvalidated. The original BeatStates package remains unchanged.

Phase-aware murmur prototype1.9(14),2026-11-14: ML/PhaseModels/Murmur.mlpackage is our own multi-task temporal model plus a preserved-v1 positive-evidence branch. Public CirCor TSV labels supervise S1/systole/S2/diastole; source state0 is masked, not declared healthy/noise. Weak early/mid/late/holo murmur timing is derived from source metadata, with missing timing and uncertain labels masked. No code or weights from the McDonald/Gales/Agarwal2024 GRU/HSMM paper were copied; that paper informed the use of explicit timing supervision (https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pdig.0000436). Our implementation uses temporal convolutions, phase pooling, weak timing, training-only hard negatives and a fixed evidence combination. CirCor ODC Attribution1.0 continues to apply; bundled sample source/license files are unchanged. The two noise clips retain the attribution above. No private audio, Watch data or additional personal recordings train/evaluate this model. No PASCAL audio was downloaded or used.
