BPM improvement protocol — November 14, 2026

Purpose
Improve the phone-only BPM estimator's useful coverage, resistance to incorrect rates,
and recovery after noise. Watch readings remain evaluation references; they never
enter feature extraction, model inference, beat selection or smoothing. No AFib or
murmur classifier is implemented by this work. Finite tests cannot establish 100%
accuracy or clinical reliability.

Development and evaluation boundaries
The saved personal series is local feasibility/development evidence. Its summary
outcomes have already been inspected, so no part of that series is described as a
genuinely unseen final test. Keep stopped attempts, rejected readings and participant
annotations. Raw audio, derived traces and individual results stay in ignored,
private diagnostic-logs directories. Do not upload these recordings or include them
in a hackathon submission without separately establishing eligibility and consent.

An additional public cohort is reserved by ML/reserve_validation.py using metadata
only. Linked patient IDs are resolved before excluding every earlier training,
development and test group. The seed and checksums make the reservation reproducible.
Do not download or inspect the new waveforms/annotations until the final source and
model hashes are frozen. The download tool verifies that freeze. Report the complete
cohort, missing references, negative controls and every large error. Noise mixtures
reuse known noise sources; new patients alone do not establish unseen-noise validity.

The user's final prospective device validation is exactly two cycles/eight tests:
quiet, background sound, brief repositioning and off chest, twice each, in rotating
order. Retain the exact tested algorithm/model hashes. Do not tune between cycles.
No further personal recording rounds are assumed or promised by this protocol.

Replay
Tools/prepare_audio_replay.py produces native audio-only inputs separately from
evaluation-only Watch references. Tools/AudioReplay.swift runs the actual saved WAV
through native Core ML and feature extraction. Tools/TimingReplay.swift evaluates
timing changes on cached states and original energy/mask traces. Record all source,
model, waveform and output hashes. First compare the unchanged replay with the live
decisions: saved PCM16 quantization and per-update maximum motion are approximations
to live floating-point samples and the unsaved 20ms motion history. Measure their
effect before using replay comparisons. Do not join sample times across masked gaps.

Metrics
Report accepted and rejected update counts, long gaps, first usable estimate,
recovery after noise, false BPM returns in all off-chest updates, and the number and
size of large differences. Overlapping updates are not independent trials. Include
per-trial and per-condition results; a small average on selected returned readings
alone is insufficient. Match unique Watch sample timestamps, retain absent phone
outputs, and show common-pair and newly returned comparisons separately. A Watch
sample is not ECG ground truth and its internal averaging window is not specified.

For public S1 annotations, retain the historical eight-second median-interval
reference for regression comparisons. Also report the fixed recent-four-interval
reference, identical for every estimator: 240 divided by the time between the last
five annotated S1 onsets. Require five nonnegative-time onsets and a newest annotation
within 1.5 median intervals; otherwise label that reference missing. This second
definition measures a shorter recent rate. Report both definitions rather than
choosing whichever makes a candidate look better. State that public annotations are
semi-supervised timing references from different recording equipment, not simultaneous
ECG on this phone. Keep missing and variable-interval recordings visible.

Research basis
Springer, Tarassenko and Clifford, Logistic Regression-HSMM-based Heart Sound
Segmentation: https://moody-challenge.physionet.org/2022/refs/LR_HSMM_Segmentation_IEEE_Springer_Tarassenko_Clifford.pdf
The paper supports checking sound ordering and duration under noisy conditions.
No reference implementation or trained Springer weights were copied.
Apple HealthKit sample timestamps: https://developer.apple.com/documentation/healthkit/hksample
Apple workout sampling: https://developer.apple.com/documentation/healthkit/running-workout-sessions
Dataset/license/training provenance remain in ML/PROVENANCE.txt.
