Murmur prototype — methods, November 14, 2026

Target
Classify sounds in a recording as Absent, Present or Unknown/unsure. These are
experimental murmur-pattern labels, not diagnoses of valve disease or AFib.
Negative audio does not rule out a murmur elsewhere or heart disease. BPM timing
and Apple Watch readings never enter the murmur model or decide its eligibility.

Data and split

The public release contains 942 patient/visit rows and 3,163 recordings. Resolving
Additional ID links produces 872 distinct patient groups. All visits, locations
and derivatives of a group remain in the same split. The metadata-only manifest
uses seed murmur-v1-21142026, source checksums and fixed label-stratified quotas.

Training:   652 linked groups, 702 patient/visit rows, 2,356 recordings.
Validation: 100 linked groups, 113 patient/visit rows,   367 recordings.
Final test: 120 linked groups, 127 patient/visit rows,   440 recordings.

Final-test groups exclude every group whose audio had been accessed during earlier
BPM work (156 groups). Neither final-test waveforms nor their features are read by
training, checkpoint selection or sample selection. All development downloads match
the published checksums. Test download requires the final model/source/metric freeze.

For a Present patient, only recordings at an annotated murmur location have target
Present; recordings at other annotated recording locations have target Absent.
Unknown murmur annotations stay Unknown. They are not declared healthy or treated
as definite artifact. Patient/visit labels are retained separately for aggregation.
No private audio, personal recordings, Watch measurements or clinical outcome
labels are used for training. This model is trained from scratch.

Model
Four seconds of mono audio at 2,000 Hz. Fixed 256-sample Hann-window Fourier filters,
40-sample hop, 32 mel bands spanning 25 to 950 Hz, log power. Fourier filtering,
mel projection and normalization are included in the exported Core ML model, so
the phone receives waveform samples directly. The small temporal convolutional
network pools time features and predicts three sound-label scores. Scores are not
calibrated probabilities of disease and are not displayed as medical confidence.

Training uses 24 epochs of 100 steps, seed 2114, balanced sampling of the original
three location labels with additional known synthetic/recorded artifacts assigned
Unknown. Gentle log-energy perturbations and limited frequency masking preserve
the positive label. Validation balanced recall chooses the checkpoint. Fixed
decision: Absent or Present only if its winning mean score is at least 0.60;
otherwise Unsure. No threshold tuning is performed on the final test.

Native inference
AVAudioFile/AVAudioConverter read at most 60 seconds and convert to mono 2,000 Hz.
Nonoverlapping four-second windows plus a final complete frame-aligned window are
evaluated; the final window can overlap. Incomplete, nonfinite, nearly silent or
heavily clipped windows cannot supply an Absent/Present result. Other noise may
still fool the model. Scores are averaged across usable windows. Empty input is
Unsure. There is no heartbeat-regularity gate and no aggressive beat-only denoiser.

Evaluation
The same native Swift/Core ML code used by the app evaluates validation and final
test audio, including native resampling, windowing and gates. Report every record,
three-class confusion matrices, class recall, positive-as-Absent misses, positive
Unsure, negative-as-Present false alarms and negative Unsure. Sensitivity and
specificity denominators retain uncertain cases, preventing selective reporting
only on returned decisive results. Compare an always-Absent majority baseline.

Patient/visit aggregation: any Present recording gives Present; otherwise any
Unsure gives Unsure; otherwise Absent. This matches a multiple-location experiment,
not proof that one phone position covers every valve. Related visits remain
dependent observations. Twenty negative controls per split cover silence, hum,
white noise, synthetic contact and known speech/babble. The speech sources are
reused development assets, so this is not unseen-noise or clinical validation.

Public demo samples
Three validation recordings are selected alphabetically, one per location label,
before checking model correctness. First 20 seconds are bundled as PCM16. Exact
original/bundled checksums, recording IDs, selection and attribution are retained
in MurmurSamples/sources.json. No public or personal file is uploaded by the app.

Phone use and limits
The optional 20-second microphone capture is a feasibility feature, not validated
phone screening. Temporary audio is deleted after analysis, cancellation or normal
interruption/background handling; stale temporary captures are removed next time
the screen initializes after a terminated process. Imported audio stays in its
original location. Analysis runs on-device. The live recording path has not been
physically tested in this phase because the participant requested no more tests.

CirCor is primarily pediatric/young-person audio recorded using digital stethoscopes.
Bare-phone transfer, other ages, devices, positions, noise and subtle murmurs remain
unvalidated. Murmur detection does not determine cause, severity or clinical outcome.
This project is a prototype and is not medical advice.

the final test. Cache and detailed outputs remain ignored. Future tuning needs new
evaluation data; the final cohort is consumed once its results are inspected.

Selected checkpoint: epoch 9 of 24, by the prespecified validation balanced recall.

Final test: 440 recordings from 127 patient/visit rows, grouped into 120 previously
unread people. Rows below are reference labels; columns are predicted Absent,
Present, Unsure. All recordings and uncertain cases are included.

                    Absent   Present   Unsure
Reference Absent       234        10       77
Reference Present        6        75       23
Reference Unknown        5         0       10

75/104 murmur-positive recordings are flagged (72.12%); 6 receive Absent and 23
receive Unsure. Among 321 negative recordings, 234 receive Absent, 10 are false
alarms, and 77 receive Unsure. All-class accuracy is 72.50%; class-balanced recall
is 70.56%. The always-Absent baseline obtains 72.95% raw accuracy but detects 0/104
positive recordings and has only 33.33% class-balanced recall. Raw accuracy alone
is therefore a poor summary of this imbalanced task. Unsure occurs in 110/440 cases
(25.0%); it is never removed from denominators to inflate the headline result.

29/37 positive patient/visits are flagged; one receives Absent and seven Unsure.
