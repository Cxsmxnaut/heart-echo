Candidate A
Initialize the temporal feature network and global murmur head from the exact
frozen v1 checkpoint. Add a five-class phase head and a murmur residual head that
pools learned features separately using the predicted S1/systole/S2/diastole
probabilities. The residual head starts at zero, preserving v1 class outputs
exactly before training. Ground-truth phases are never supplied during inference.

For the first three epochs, freeze the old feature network/global head while the
new phase/residual heads learn. Then jointly train all student parameters. Loss:
murmur cross-entropy +0.5phase cross-entropy +0.2teacher distillation, temperature2.
The frozen teacher helps retain prior sound patterns. Fixed24epochs,100steps,
48examples/batch,AdamW learning rate0.00015,seed2414. Exact settings and source
hashes are stored with the separate candidate weights under ignored ML/generated.

Checkpoint selection is fixed before training: maximize positive detection, then
class-balanced recall, among checkpoints with a false-alarm-rate increase no more
than2percentagepoints, unsure-rate increase no more than5points and positive-as-
Absent-rate increase no more than2points versus epoch0 on the same selection data.
The original epoch0 is included as a fallback and cannot qualify as an improvement.
These are engineering comparison bounds, not medical acceptance criteria.
